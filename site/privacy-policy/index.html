Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-06

1. Who we are

FrogPop: Bubble Quest ("the game", package com.poyraz.frogpop) is published by Poyraz Yazılım ("we"). Contact: omerr.poyrazz@gmail.com.

2. Short version

The game works fully offline. We run no servers, no accounts and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device. The only third-party data processing happens through the advertising and billing services of Google/Apple described below, and only in builds that include them.

3. Data stored on your device

The game stores a small save file in the app's private storage: high score, total score, games played, play time, coins, unlocked costumes and worlds, achievements, daily-reward dates, settings (volume, vibration, quality, language), power-up counts and PRO status.

This file never leaves your device through our code. We cannot see it. It is removed when you use Settings > Delete my progress or uninstall the game. A rolling local backup copy is kept next to it for crash recovery.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Free players may see ads served by Google AdMob (interstitial ads between games and optional rewarded ads). AdMob is operated by Google LLC and may collect and process, as a separate controller/processor under Google's own terms: the advertising ID, IP address, device and OS information, app and ad interaction data and approximate location derived from the IP address, in order to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and (where permitted) personalize ads.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions the game shows Google's consent message before personalised ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Android: Settings > Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking / Apple Advertising).

More information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads and https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.

Note about builds: the open project files do not bundle the AdMob SDK. In a build without the SDK no ad is shown and no advertising data is processed. PRO users and users who removed ads see no ads.

5. Purchases (PRO, remove ads)

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never receive your payment card or account details. The game only stores locally whether PRO / ad-free is active. Because the game has no server, purchases are not verified by a server of ours. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your store account settings; restoring purchases is done through the store.

6. Children

The game is aimed at a general audience of players aged 13 and older and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad requests are configured with child-directed tags disabled because the game is not child-directed; if you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and use your device's parental controls or ad-personalisation settings.

7. Third-party services

Google AdMob (ads) - Google LLC. Google Play Billing (Android purchases) - Google LLC. App Store In-App Purchase (iOS purchases) - Apple Inc. The game engine (Godot, MIT license) does not transmit data. No other third-party SDKs are used.

8. Data deletion and your rights

Because we hold no personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to export or erase remotely. You can delete all local data at any time: Settings > Delete my progress, or uninstall the game. To manage data held by Google or Apple (advertising ID, purchase history), use their privacy controls. Residents of the EEA/UK (GDPR) and Türkiye (KVKK) may contact us with any question or request at the address below and we will answer within 30 days.

9. Security

The save file is stored in the app sandbox, protected by the operating system, with an integrity checksum against corruption. No transmission of this file occurs.

10. Changes

If the way the game processes data changes (for example when an SDK is added), we will update this page and the effective date before releasing that version.

11. Contact
